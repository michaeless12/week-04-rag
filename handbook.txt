
# Employee Handbook

## Working Hours
Employees work Monday to Friday, from 8am to 5pm. There is a one-hour lunch break between noon and 2pm.

## Annual Leave
Employees are entitled to 20 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests must be submitted to HR at least two weeks in advance.

## Sick Leave
Employees must notify their supervisor before 9am when absent due to illness. A medical certificate may be required for absences exceeding three consecutive working days.

## Performance Reviews
Performance reviews take place twice yearly, in June and December. Employees are evaluated based on productivity, teamwork, communication, punctuality and assigned objectives.

## Salary Payments
Salaries are paid on the last working day of each month, directly into employees' registered bank accounts.

## Remote Work
Employees may request remote work arrangements from their department managers. Approval depends on job responsibilities and departmental needs.

## Training and Development
Employees have access to professional development opportunities. They may request approval to attend relevant workshops, seminars and training programmes.
